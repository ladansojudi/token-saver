# Interview Guide v1 — Shelfwise receiving flow

## Intro script
"Thanks for making time. I'd like to hear how deliveries are handled in your shop, from when they arrive until everything's recorded. There are no right or wrong answers; we're learning about the work, not testing you. Is it OK if I take notes and record audio, just for our team?"

Source: brief.md, Research questions 1–4 (the ones tagged "users").
Ask about real, recent events, not opinions in general. If something has never happened to the participant, skip the question rather than asking them to imagine it.

## RQ1 — In which step do mistakes or delays happen when receiving a delivery?
1. Walk me through the last delivery you received, from the moment it arrived until the stock was updated in the app.
2. Which part of that took the most time or effort? What was going on at that point?
3. Tell me about a time receiving a delivery didn't go the way you expected. What happened?

## RQ2 — Where do differences come from, and how are they fixed once found?
1. Think of the last time what arrived didn't match what you ordered. How did you first notice?
2. What did you do next? Who was involved, and where did you record it, in the app or anywhere else?
3. Tell me about a difference you found only after the delivery was already confirmed. How was it handled?

## RQ3 — What limits does counting at the shelf put on recording in the app?
1. Describe how you count a delivery: where you are, and what you use to keep track of the numbers.
2. Once you've finished counting, what do you do with the numbers? Talk me through it.
3. Tell me about a time a count got lost, changed or had to be redone between the shelf and the computer.

## RQ4 — Why is recorded stock different from real stock, and how much do users trust it?
1. Tell me about the last time the app showed a different amount from what was really on the shelf. What do you think caused it?
2. When you need to know how much of an item you have, what do you check first?
3. How do you decide when to go with the number in the app and when to check for yourself?
