## 1. In which step do mistakes or delays happen when receiving a delivery?

**Status:** Partly
**External research:** None
**User evidence**
- Step 4 is confusing. P1: "I never know if I should edit the order or the stock."
- The order of the steps changes when the shop is busy. P1 signed for a delivery before counting it.
- Recording waits until the shop is quiet (P2). The delays come from serving customers, not from the app.

**Still unknown**
- Whether step 4 confuses other users too, or only P1.
- How long the delays really are, and whether they lead to mistakes.

## 2. Where do differences come from, and how are they fixed once found?

**Status:** Partly
**External research:** None
**User evidence**
- The supplier sent fewer than the driver's note said: 58 packs instead of 60 (P2).
- Nobody keeps a record of a difference that anyone else uses. P1 edits the number with no reason. P2's comments go unread. P2's written count went away with the driver.

**Still unknown**
- Items that arrive without being ordered. Nobody described one.
- How the supplier settles differences, for example credit notes or re-delivery.
- Whether differences also come from counting or entry mistakes, not only from suppliers.

## 3. What limits does counting at the shelf put on recording in the app?

**Status:** Partly
**External research:** None
**User evidence**
- Both participants count on paper and enter the numbers later, from 20 minutes later up to the end of the day.
- P1: types them in "at the end of the day, if I remember".
- P2 counts in the stockroom, not at the shelf. This goes against Known 6.

**Still unknown**
- What really happens at the shelf. Nobody has been observed counting yet.
- How often counts are lost or forgotten.

## 4. Why is recorded stock different from real stock, and how much do users trust it?

**Status:** Partly. Trust has some evidence. The "why" is mostly my inference.
**External research:** None
**User evidence**
- Trust depends on the kind of item. P1 distrusts pasta and rice and checks the shelf. P2: "Small items are always off by a few."
- Possible causes, which I inferred and nobody said: late or forgotten entry, edits without a reason, notes nobody reads.

**Still unknown**
- Real causes, confirmed by users or by data. Known 7 says there is no reliable data.
- How much of the difference comes from receiving deliveries rather than sales, spoilage or theft, which are out of scope.
