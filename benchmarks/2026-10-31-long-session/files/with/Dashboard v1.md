# Shelfwise evidence dashboard v1

Sources: brief.md, Interview Synthesis v1.md (2 participants, P1 and P2). No desk research has been done yet.
Status key: **answered** means enough evidence to act on; **partly** means early signals that need more participants or sources; **open** means no evidence yet.

| Research question | Status | External research | User evidence | Still unknown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RQ1: Which step has mistakes or delays? | Partly | None yet | Recording is delayed by busy hours (P1, P2). Step 4 is confusing: "edit the order or the stock" (P1). P1 signed for the delivery before counting. Steps 2 and 3 happen in different places (P2). | Whether this pattern holds beyond 2 people. Which step causes the most errors, not just delays. |
| RQ2: Where do differences come from, and how are they fixed? | Partly | None yet | Short deliveries (P2: 60 ordered, 58 counted). Fixes leave no findable reason: written on the driver's note (P2), number edited without a reason (P1), comment box "nobody reads" (P2). | Other sources, such as wrong items or items that were never ordered (the brief's scenario). What happens with the supplier afterwards. |
| RQ3: Limits of counting at the shelf on recording | Partly | None yet | Counts go onto paper and are typed in later, from 20 minutes to the end of the day (P1, P2). P2 counts in the stockroom, not at the shelf. | How often counts are lost or mistyped. Whether Known 6 (counting happens at the shelf) is true. |
| RQ4: Why is stock wrong, and how much is it trusted? | Partly | None yet | Trust depends on the item: no trust for pasta or rice (P1), small items "always off by a few" (P2). Both check the shelf. | Why those items are off. Our possible causes come from linking interview answers, not from what participants said. |
| RQ5: How do other stock apps handle receiving? | Open | None yet | Indirect only: P1's earlier spreadsheet was "slower but I knew what I typed". | Everything. Needs a review of competing tools. |
| RQ6: What does published research say? | Open | None yet | Not applicable | Everything. Needs desk research. |
