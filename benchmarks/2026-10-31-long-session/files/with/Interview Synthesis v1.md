# Shelfwise interview synthesis v1

Sources: interview-notes-p1.md (P1, grocery owner, in person, 30 min, 2026-10-20) and interview-notes-p2.md (P2, stationery stock keeper, video call, 25 min, 2026-10-22).
Only 2 participants, so these are signals to check, not findings. Quotes are taken word for word from the notes.

## RQ1: In which step do mistakes or delays happen when receiving a delivery?
- **Deliveries arrive when staff are busy, so recording gets pushed back.** Both participants delay the work. P2 says recording waits until the shop is quiet. P1: "The driver came at 8, I was alone at the till, so I signed the paper first and counted later."
- **The order is confirmed before anything is counted.** P1 signed for the delivery before counting it (quote above).
- **Step 4 (record differences) is confusing.** P1: "I never know if I should edit the order or the stock."
- **Steps 2 and 3 happen in different places.** P2 counts in one place and compares at the computer, "so I keep a paper in my pocket".

## RQ2: Where do differences come from, and how are they fixed once found?
- **Short deliveries are one source.** P2: "The driver's note said 60 packs of pens, I counted 58."
- **Fixes are made outside the app, or without a reason.**
  - P2: "I wrote 58 on the driver note and he took it away." The only record left the shop.
  - P1: "When something is short I just change the number in the app. I don't write down why."
  - P2: "I put the difference in the comment box but nobody reads it."
- **Pattern:** neither participant leaves a record of why a difference happened that anyone can find later. This fits Known 7 (no reliable data on why differences happen).

## RQ3: What limits does counting at the shelf put on recording in the app?
- **Counts go onto paper first and are typed in later.**
  - P1 uses a notepad and types the numbers in "at the end of the day, if I remember".
  - P2 walks to the computer "maybe 20 minutes later, after serving customers".
- **The gap between counting and recording depends on memory and spare time.**
- **Counting doesn't always happen at the shelf.** P2 counts in the stockroom, which questions Known 6 ("counting usually happens at the shelf").

## RQ4: Why is recorded stock different from real stock, and how much do users trust it?
- **Trust depends on the item.**
  - P1: "I don't trust the stock number for pasta or rice. I go and look at the shelf."
  - P2: "The numbers are fine for big items. Small items are always off by a few."
- **Possible causes, from RQ1–3:** late or forgotten entry, edits with no reason recorded, and differences that are written on the driver's note or in an unread comment. These are links we've drawn, not something participants said.

## Outside the four questions
- **Earlier tool:** P1 used a spreadsheet before: "the spreadsheet was slower but I knew what I typed." This may be useful for RQ5 (other tools).

## Open questions for the next round
