# Shelfwise brief (made-up project for a design course)

## Company and app
Shelfwise makes a desktop app that small shops (grocery, stationery) use to track stock in the stockroom and on the shelves. The owner or the stock keeper uses it, often on a computer at the till or in the stockroom.

## Why now
Users say the stock in the app is wrong, or that numbers don't match after a delivery. Nobody knows yet whether the problem is the delivery, the counting, recording in the app, or something else.

## Our scope
Only the flow for receiving a delivery from a supplier and recording it in stock. Sales, reports and ordering are out of scope.

## Scenario (made up, not evidence)
Reza, a stock keeper, counts the boxes at the shelf, then opens the purchase order at the computer. One item arrived short and another was never ordered. He isn't sure where to record the difference.

## Current flow
1. Open the purchase order 2. Count what arrived 3. Compare with the order 4. Record differences 5. Confirm and update stock

## What we know (company's claims, not checked)
- Known 1: The app only runs on desktop.
- Known 2: Users are shop owners or stock keepers.
- Known 3: Receiving a delivery has five steps.
- Known 4: Sometimes the goods that arrive don't match the order.
- Known 5: Recorded stock is sometimes different from real stock.
- Known 6: Counting usually happens at the shelf, not at the desk.
- Known 7: There is no reliable data on why differences happen.

## Research questions
- Research question 1: In which step do mistakes or delays happen when receiving a delivery? (users)
- Research question 2: Where do differences come from, and how are they fixed once found? (users)
- Research question 3: What limits does counting at the shelf put on recording in the app? (users)
- Research question 4: Why is recorded stock different from real stock, and how much do users trust it? (users)
- Research question 5: How do other stock apps handle receiving deliveries? (other tools)
- Research question 6: What does published research say about stock accuracy and delivery differences? (published sources)
