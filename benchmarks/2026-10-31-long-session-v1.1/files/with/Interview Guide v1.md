# Interview Guide v1

Draft open questions for research questions 1–4 (see brief.md, "Research questions"). These are questions to ask users, not findings.

## Intro script

"Thanks for making time to talk with me. I'd like to hear how receiving deliveries works in your shop day to day. We're looking at the app, not testing you, so there are no right or wrong answers."

## Research question 1: Where mistakes or delays happen when receiving a delivery

1. Tell me about the last delivery you received. What happened from the moment it arrived until you were done with it?
2. Which part of receiving a delivery takes you the longest, and why do you think that is?
3. Can you remember a time something went wrong while you were receiving a delivery? Walk me through what happened.

## Research question 2: Where differences come from and how they are fixed

1. Tell me about the last time the goods that arrived didn't match what you expected. How did you notice?
2. When you find a difference, what do you do next, and who else gets involved?
3. How do you usually find out where a difference came from? What makes that easy or hard?

## Research question 3: How counting at the shelf limits recording in the app

1. Where do you usually count a delivery, and where are you when you record it in the app?
2. Walk me through what you do with the numbers once you've finished counting.
3. Tell me about a time something got lost or changed between counting and recording. What happened?

## Research question 4: Why recorded stock differs from real stock, and how much users trust it

1. Tell me about the last time the stock in the app didn't match what was on the shelf. What did you think had happened?
2. When you need to know how much of something you have, what do you check first, and why?
3. How do you decide when to rely on the numbers in the app and when to check for yourself?
