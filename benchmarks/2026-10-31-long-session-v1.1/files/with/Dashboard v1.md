## RQ1: Where mistakes or delays happen

- **User evidence:** Deliveries arrive during busy hours, so people sign before counting (P1) and recording waits until the shop is quiet (P2). Step 4 confuses P1 ("edit the order or the stock"). See Interview Synthesis v1.md, RQ1.
- **Still unknown:** Whether this is common across shops. Whether steps 1 and 5 cause problems. How often deliveries arrive at busy times.

## RQ2: Where differences come from and how they're fixed

- **User evidence:** People do find differences, but the record of them gets lost: it leaves with the driver's note (P2), gets changed without a reason (P1), or goes in a comment box nobody reads (P2). See Interview Synthesis v1.md, RQ2.
- **Still unknown:** Whether differences start with the supplier, the count or the data entry. What happens to the corrected driver's note afterwards. Who, if anyone, is supposed to read the comment box.

## RQ3: How counting away from the computer limits recording

- **User evidence:** Both participants count away from the computer and carry the numbers on paper. They enter them 20 minutes later (P2) or at the end of the day, "if I remember" (P1). P2 counts in the stockroom, which partly contradicts Known 6. See Interview Synthesis v1.md, RQ3.
- **Still unknown:** How often counts get lost or mistyped between paper and app. Where other users count.

## RQ4: Why recorded stock differs, and how much users trust it

- **User evidence:** Trust depends on the item. Both participants distrust the numbers for small or high-volume items, and P1 checks the shelf instead. See Interview Synthesis v1.md, RQ4.
- **Still unknown:** Why. Neither participant said, and linking it to RQ2 and RQ3 is our interpretation. How big the gap actually is (Known 7: no reliable data).
